## ✍️ Article Structure Template

Use this structure for all articles:

### 1. **Title (H1)**
```
Format: [Keyword] | [Unique Angle] | [Year if relevant]

Examples:
- "Finding Affordable Mental Health Therapy Near Me: Complete 2025 Guide"
- "10 Mindfulness Practices to Combat Loneliness (That Actually Work)"
- "How to Cope with Emotional Exhaustion: Evidence-Based Strategies"

Rules:
- Include focus keyword naturally
- 50-60 characters ideal
- Make it compelling, not just keyword-stuffed
```

### 2. **Introduction (100-150 words)**
```
Paragraph 1: Hook - Address the pain point directly
- "If you're searching for affordable mental health therapy, you're not alone.
  78% of Americans report cost as the #1 barrier to getting help."

Paragraph 2: Promise - What they'll learn
- "In this guide, you'll discover 7 proven ways to access therapy on any budget,
  including free resources, sliding scale options, and insurance alternatives."

Paragraph 3: Credibility - Why trust this
- "All recommendations are clinically reviewed and include real cost data from
  2025 provider surveys."

MUST: Include focus keyword in first 100 words
```

### 3. **Table of Contents (Optional but Recommended)**
```html
<div class="bg-gray-50 p-6 rounded-lg mb-8">
  <h2 class="font-bold mb-4">In This Article:</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="#section1">Why Therapy Costs So Much</a></li>
    <li><a href="#section2">7 Ways to Find Affordable Care</a></li>
    <li><a href="#section3">How to Ask for Sliding Scale Fees</a></li>
  </ul>
</div>
```

### 4. **Main Content Sections (H2s)**
```
Use 4-7 H2 sections:

Example for "Finding Affordable Therapy":
H2: Why Is Mental Health Therapy So Expensive?
H2: 7 Ways to Access Affordable Mental Health Care
H2: How to Find Sliding Scale Therapists Near You
H2: Using Insurance to Your Advantage
H2: Free Alternatives to Traditional Therapy
H2: Questions to Ask When Calling Therapists
H2: What to Do If You're Denied Care

Rules:
- Include keyword variants in 2-3 H2s
- Make H2s descriptive and skimmable
- Each H2 should be 200-400 words
```

### 5. **Subsections (H3s)**
```
Break down H2s with H3s for readability:

H2: 7 Ways to Access Affordable Mental Health Care
  H3: 1. Community Mental Health Centers
  H3: 2. University Counseling Programs
  H3: 3. Open Path Collective
  ...

Each H3 should include:
- What it is
- How much it costs
- How to access it
- Pros/cons or tips
```

### 6. **Multimedia Elements**
```
Include every 300-400 words:

- Callout boxes (tips, warnings, examples)
- Numbered/bulleted lists
- Tables (comparisons, pricing)
- Images (infographics, screenshots) [if available]
- Quotes from experts or users

Example callout:
<div class="bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500 p-4 my-6">
  <strong>Pro Tip:</strong> When calling therapists about sliding scale fees,
  ask: "Do you have any availability for clients on a sliding scale basis?"
  This phrasing works better than "Do you offer sliding scale?"
</div>
```

### 7. **Internal Links**
```
Link to relevant pages 3-5 times per article:

- Link to support category pages
- Link to related blog articles
- Link to app signup page
- Link to dashboard features

Example:
"If cost is preventing you from seeking help, check out our
[complete guide to affordable mental health care](/support/affordable-mental-health-care)
or [start using our free mental wellness app](/auth/signup) today."
```

### 8. **FAQ Section**
```html
<section class="bg-gray-50 p-8 rounded-lg my-12">
  <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mb-6">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

  <div class="space-y-6">

```

```html

### 10. **Conclusion (100-150 words)**
```
Paragraph 1: Summarize key takeaways
- "Finding affordable mental health therapy takes persistence, but it's possible.
  Community mental health centers, sliding scale options, and free resources like
  our app make quality care accessible."

Paragraph 2: Call to action
- "Start by calling your local community mental health center and asking about
  sliding scale fees. While you wait for your first appointment, use our free

  regardless of your budget."

## 🎨 Writing Style Guide

### Tone:
- **Empathetic**: Acknowledge pain points without being condescending
- **Hopeful**: Always offer solutions and paths forward
- **Authoritative**: Back claims with research, but stay accessible
- **Conversational**: Write like talking to a friend, not a textbook

### Voice:
- **Second person ("you")** for engagement
- **Active voice** over passive ("Use this tool" vs. "This tool can be used")
- **Short sentences** (15-20 words average)
- **Short paragraphs** (2-4 sentences max)

### Word Choice:
- **Use**: free, affordable, accessible, evidence-based, proven, effective
- **Avoid**: cheap, impossible, always/never, guaranteed cure
- **Replace jargon**: "CBT" → "Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)"
- **Be specific**: "1-800-662-4357" not "call the helpline"

## ✅ E-E-A-T Compliance (Google Quality Guidelines)

### Experience
- Include real examples and case studies
- Reference user feedback or testimonials
- Describe personal or observed experiences with methods

### Expertise
- Cite research studies and statistics
- Link to authoritative sources (NIH, NIMH, APA)
- Note when content is clinically reviewed

### Authoritativeness
- Display author credentials
- Link to author bio page
- Show organizational expertise

### Trustworthiness
- Include medical disclaimers
- Be transparent about limitations
- Update content regularly (add date last updated)
- Correct errors publicly

### Example Disclaimer:
```html
<div class="bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-400 p-4 my-6">
  <p class="text-sm text-yellow-800">
    <strong>Medical Disclaimer:</strong> This article is for informational purposes only
    and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified mental health
    professional for diagnosis and treatment recommendations.
  </p>
</div>
```
