## Content Creation Guidelines

### Article Structure Template:
1. **Title (H1)**: Include focus keyword at beginning
2. **Introduction**: Include focus keyword in first 100 words
3. **Problem Statement**: Address user pain point
4. **Solution Section**: Provide actionable advice
5. **Tool/App Integration**: Mention your app features naturally
6. **Clinical References**: Include evidence-based support
7. **FAQ Section**: Answer common questions
8. **Conclusion**: Summarize key points with CTA

### SEO Best Practices:
- **Word Count**: 1,500-2,500 words for comprehensive coverage
- **Keyword Density**: 1-2% natural keyword usage
- **Internal Links**: 3-5 relevant internal links
- **External Links**: 2-3 authoritative medical sources
- **Image Optimization**: Alt text with keywords, compressed images
- **Readability**: Short paragraphs, bullet points, clear headings

### Content Publishing Schedule:
- **Publish Day**: Every Tuesday and Thursday
- **Promotion**: Same-day social media promotion
- **Internal Linking**: Update 2-3 old articles with new links
- **Repurposing**: Create social media snippets, email newsletter content

## Measurement & Optimization

### Key Metrics to Track:
1. **Organic Traffic Growth**: Month-over-month increase
2. **Keyword Rankings**: Position tracking for target keywords
3. **Time on Page**: User engagement with content
4. **Conversion Rate**: Signups from blog traffic
5. **Backlinks Acquired**: Authority building through content

### Monthly Review Process:
1. **Performance Analysis**: Review top and bottom performing articles
2. **Keyword Adjustment**: Update based on ranking changes
3. **Content Refresh**: Update high-performing old content
4. **Promotion Boost**: Additional promotion for underperforming content
5. **Topic Planning**: Adjust upcoming content based on results
