# Long-Tail Keyword Strategy for Mental Wellness App

## Overview

This document outlines the strategic approach for targeting long-tail keywords across 10 core categories that address the pain points of mental health and mindfulness communities. These keywords are designed to capture users at different stages of their mental health journey and provide highly relevant, evidence-based content.

## Why Long-Tail Keywords?

Based on 2025 SEO best practices and healthcare industry research:

- **Higher Conversion Rates**: Long-tail keywords attract users with specific intent (3-5x higher conversion than generic terms)
- **Lower Competition**: Easier to rank in search results compared to broad keywords
- **Better User Experience**: Content matches exact user needs and search intent
- **Improved E-E-A-T Signals**: Specific, expert content strengthens Google's Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness signals
- **Voice Search Optimization**: Natural language queries align with voice search patterns

## Implementation Checklist for Content Writers

When creating content targeting these keywords:

### ✅ Before Writing

1. **Select Focus Keyword**: Choose ONE primary long-tail keyword from the category above
2. **Choose Content Type**: Determine if this is an Article, FAQ, or How-To guide
3. **Gather Related Keywords**: Select 3-5 related keywords from the category list
4. **Define Target Audience**: Who is experiencing this specific pain point?

### ✅ During Writing

5. **Title Optimization**: Include focus keyword in H1 (preferably at the beginning)
6. **First 100 Words**: Naturally mention the focus keyword within the opening paragraph
7. **Subheadings (H2/H3)**: Include semantic variations of keywords in at least 2 subheadings
8. **Keyword Density**: Aim for 1-2% natural keyword density (don't force it)
9. **Content Length**: Target 1,500-2,500 words for comprehensive coverage
10. **Internal Keywords**: Add focus keyword and related keywords in Sanity CMS fields

### ✅ After Writing

11. **Meta Title**: Include focus keyword (50-60 characters)
12. **Meta Description**: Include focus keyword naturally (140-160 characters)
13. **Alt Text**: Include relevant keywords in image alt text when appropriate
14. **Clinical Review**: Ensure content is reviewed by qualified professionals
15. **Structured Data**: Verify correct schema type is selected (Article/FAQ/HowTo)

## CMS Implementation Guide

### In Sanity Studio:

1. **Content Type Field**: Select from:
   - Article (default) → Generates `MedicalScholarlyArticle` schema
   - FAQ / Q&A → Generates `FAQPage` schema
   - How-To Guide → Generates `HowTo` schema

2. **Focus Keyword Field**:
   - Enter the primary long-tail keyword exactly as written above
   - Example: "how to cope with emotional exhaustion and burnout naturally"

3. **Related Keywords Field**:
   - Add 3-7 related keywords as tags
   - These support semantic SEO and content relevance

4. **Topics Field**:
   - Broader category tags (e.g., "Burnout", "Anxiety", "Mindfulness")
   - Used for content filtering and navigation

5. **SEO Metadata → Internal Keywords**:
   - Optional: Additional keywords for content planning only
   - NOT rendered as meta tags (deprecated practice)

## SEO Best Practices (2025 Standards)

### ✅ DO THIS:

- **Natural Integration**: Use keywords naturally in content, headings, and meta descriptions
- **User Intent Focus**: Answer the specific question the user is asking
- **E-E-A-T Compliance**: Include author credentials, clinical review, citations
- **Structured Data**: Always add appropriate schema markup
- **Mobile Optimization**: Ensure content is readable on all devices
- **Internal Linking**: Link to related articles using keyword anchor text
- **Long-Form Content**: Aim for 1,500+ words for comprehensive coverage

### ❌ DON'T DO THIS:

- **Keyword Stuffing**: Forcing keywords unnaturally into content
- **Meta Keywords Tag**: This is deprecated and ignored by all major search engines since 2009
- **Duplicate Content**: Each article must have unique focus keyword
- **Hidden Text**: Any form of invisible text or keyword hiding
- **Over-Optimization**: Sacrificing readability for keyword density

## Measuring Success

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

1. **Organic Search Rankings**: Track position for focus keywords in Google Search Console
2. **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: Monitor CTR from search results (target: 3-5%)
3. **Time on Page**: Users should spend 3+ minutes reading (indicates quality)
4. **Bounce Rate**: Target <60% bounce rate for article pages
5. **Conversion Rate**: Track newsletter signups, app downloads from article pages
6. **Rich Snippets**: Monitor appearance in FAQ/HowTo rich results

### Tools:

- Google Search Console (keyword rankings, CTR, impressions)
- Google Analytics 4 (engagement metrics, conversion tracking)
- Sanity Analytics (content performance, popular topics)
