## 📋 Article Fields Guide

### **Content Tab** (Required)

#### 1. **Title** (Required)
- **Length:** 8-120 characters
- **Best Practice:** Clear, descriptive, SEO-friendly
- **Examples:**
  - ✅ "5 Proven Techniques to Manage Anxiety at Work"
  - ✅ "Understanding Depression: A Complete Guide"
  - ❌ "Anxiety" (too short)

#### 2. **Slug** (Required - Auto-generated)
- **What it is:** URL-friendly version of your title
- **Example:** "5-proven-techniques-to-manage-anxiety-at-work"
- **Action:** Click "Generate" button next to slug field
- **Result:** Article URL becomes `/blog/your-slug-here`

#### 3. **Body Content** (Required)
This is your article content. You can use:

##### **Text Blocks**
- Regular paragraphs
- **Bold text**
- *Italic text*
- Headings (H2, H3, H4)
- Bulleted lists
- Numbered lists

##### **Images**
- Click "+ Add item" → Select "Image"
- Upload image (recommended: 1200x630px for hero images)
- **Alt text** (Required): Describe the image for screen readers
- **Caption** (Optional): Credit or description

##### **External Links**
- Highlight text
- Click link icon
- Enter URL
- Add accessible label
- Set nofollow (default: checked for external links)

##### **Callout Boxes**
- Click "+ Add item" → Select "Callout"
- Use for:
  - Important notes
  - Warnings
  - Tips
  - Key takeaways

#### 4. **Hero Image** (Optional but Recommended)
- **Size:** 1200x630px (optimized for social sharing)
- **Alt text:** Required for accessibility
- **Credit:** Attribution if needed
- **Best Practice:** Use high-quality, relevant images

---

### **Metadata Tab** (Required)

#### 5. **Editorial Status** (Required)
- **Draft:** Work in progress (not published)
- **In Review:** Ready for review
- **Ready:** Approved and ready to publish

**Note:** Only articles with "Ready" status AND a published date will appear on the website.

#### 6. **Summary/Excerpt** (Required)
- **Length:** 40-220 characters
- **Purpose:** Shows on blog listing page
- **Best Practice:** Compelling summary that makes readers want to click
- **Example:** "Learn science-backed techniques to reduce workplace anxiety and improve your mental wellbeing with practical daily exercises."

#### 7. **Published At** (Required for publication)
- **Set date/time:** When the article should go live
- **Important:** Article won't show until this date/time passes
- **Best Practice:** Set to now if publishing immediately

#### 8. **Estimated Reading Time** (Optional)
- **Range:** 1-60 minutes
- **How to calculate:** ~200 words per minute
- **Example:** 1000 words = 5 minutes

#### 9. **Topics/Tags** (Optional)
- **Max:** 8 tags
- **Purpose:** Helps with categorization and SEO
- **Examples:**
  - anxiety
  - depression
  - mindfulness
  - therapy
  - wellness
  - mental-health

#### 10. **Authors** (Recommended)
- **Select from:** Authors list (create authors first)
- **Minimum:** 1 author
- **Purpose:** Gives credit and builds authority

---

### **Compliance Tab** (For Healthcare Content)

#### 11. **Clinical Review Metadata** (Optional)
Use if your content has been medically reviewed:
- **Reviewer name:** Dr. Jane Smith, MD
- **Reviewer credentials:** Board-certified psychiatrist
- **Review date:** When it was reviewed
- **Disclaimer:** Custom medical disclaimer if needed

#### 12. **HIPAA Disclaimer Override** (Optional)
- **Max:** 400 characters
- **Use:** If you need a custom HIPAA notice for this specific article
- **Default:** Uses site-wide disclaimer

---

### **Metadata Tab** (SEO)

#### 13. **SEO Metadata** (Recommended)
- **Meta Title:** 50-60 characters (shows in Google)
- **Meta Description:** 150-160 characters (shows in search results)
- **Keywords:** Comma-separated (e.g., "anxiety, mental health, coping")
- **OG Image:** Social sharing image (optional, uses hero image by default)

## 🎨 Best Practices for Writing Articles

### 1. **Content Structure**
```
Introduction (1-2 paragraphs)
├── Hook: Grab attention
└── Overview: What will they learn?

Main Content (3-5 sections)
├── Use H2 headings for main sections
├── Use H3 for subsections
├── Add images to break up text
├── Use callout boxes for key points
└── Include examples and practical tips

Conclusion (1-2 paragraphs)
├── Summarize key takeaways
└── Call to action (if appropriate)
```

### 2. **Writing Style**
- ✅ **Simple language:** Write at an 8th-grade reading level
- ✅ **Short paragraphs:** 2-3 sentences max
- ✅ **Active voice:** "Studies show" not "It has been shown"
- ✅ **Conversational:** Write like you're talking to a friend
- ✅ **Empathetic:** Remember your audience may be struggling

### 3. **SEO Optimization**
- ✅ Use your main keyword in:
  - Title
  - First paragraph
  - At least one H2 heading
  - Meta description
  - Alt text for hero image
- ✅ Add internal links to other articles
- ✅ Include external links to credible sources
- ✅ Use descriptive link text (not "click here")

### 4. **Accessibility**
- ✅ **Alt text** for ALL images
- ✅ **Heading hierarchy:** Don't skip levels (H2 → H3, not H2 → H4)
- ✅ **Link labels:** Descriptive text
- ✅ **Color contrast:** Use high-contrast text
- ✅ **Plain language:** Avoid jargon

### 5. **Healthcare Compliance**
- ✅ **Medical accuracy:** Cite credible sources
- ✅ **Clinical review:** Have content reviewed by licensed professionals
- ✅ **Disclaimers:** Include medical disclaimers
- ✅ **Evidence-based:** Reference studies and research
- ✅ **Crisis resources:** Include helpline numbers when appropriate

## 🎯 Example Article Workflow

### Creating "5 Mindfulness Techniques for Better Sleep"

1. **Open Sanity Studio** → Articles → Create
2. **Title:** "5 Mindfulness Techniques for Better Sleep"
3. **Generate Slug:** `5-mindfulness-techniques-for-better-sleep`
4. **Write Body:**
   ```
   Introduction: Why sleep matters for mental health

   ## 1. Body Scan Meditation
   [Explain technique with image]

   ## 2. Breath Counting
   [Explain technique]

   ## 3. Gratitude Journaling
   [Explain technique with callout box]

   ## 4. Progressive Muscle Relaxation
   [Explain technique]

   ## 5. Guided Sleep Meditation
   [Explain technique with audio link]

   Conclusion: Start tonight
   ```
5. **Add Hero Image:** Peaceful bedroom or meditation scene
6. **Summary:** "Improve your sleep quality with these 5 evidence-based mindfulness techniques. Simple practices you can start tonight."
7. **Published At:** [Current date/time]
8. **Reading Time:** 7 minutes
9. **Topics:** sleep, mindfulness, meditation, wellness, mental-health
10. **Author:** Select yourself
11. **SEO Title:** "5 Mindfulness Techniques for Better Sleep | Mental Wellness"
12. **SEO Description:** "Discover 5 proven mindfulness techniques to improve your sleep quality. Evidence-based practices for better rest and mental wellbeing."
13. **Status:** Ready
14. **Click "Publish"**
15. **Visit:** http://localhost:3000/blog/5-mindfulness-techniques-for-better-sleep
