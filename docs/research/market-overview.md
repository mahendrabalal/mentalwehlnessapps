# Market Overview - Mental Wellness App

## Market Definition

**Mental Wellness App Market Definition:**
- **Product Category:** Digital mental health and wellness applications
- **Core Functions:** Mood tracking, meditation, therapy sessions, crisis support, wellness coaching
- **Geographic Scope:** Primary focus on US market with global expansion considerations
- **Customer Segments:** B2C (individuals), B2B2C (employers/insurers), B2B (healthcare providers)
- **Value Chain Position:** Direct-to-consumer wellness platform with potential healthcare provider integrations

**Market Boundaries:**
- **Included:** Mobile apps, web platforms, wearable integrations for mental wellness
- **Excluded:** Traditional therapy, pharmaceutical interventions, in-person wellness services
- **Adjacent Markets:** Digital therapeutics, employee wellness platforms, telehealth

## Market Size & Growth

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- **Global Mental Health Software Market:** $6.6 billion (2024)
- **Projected Growth:** CAGR of 23.6% (2024-2029)
- **2029 Projection:** $19.9 billion globally

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
- **US Digital Mental Health Market:** $2.4 billion (2024)
- **B2C Mental Wellness Apps Segment:** $1.8 billion
- **Enterprise Wellness Solutions:** $600 million

### Market Drivers
1. **Post-pandemic mental health awareness surge**
2. **Insurance coverage expansion for digital therapeutics**
3. **Workforce wellness program adoption**
4. **Generation Z digital-first preferences**
5. **Healthcare provider digital transformation**

## Key Market Trends

### Technology Trends
- **AI-powered personalization** becoming standard
- **Wearable integration** for physiological monitoring
- **Voice-first interfaces** for accessibility
- **AR/VR therapy experiences** emerging

### Regulatory Trends
- **FDA digital therapeutics approval pathway** established
- **HIPAA compliance** becoming table stakes
- **Data privacy regulations** (CCPA, GDPR) shaping design
- **Clinical evidence requirements** increasing

### Business Model Evolution
- **Freemium to premium tiers** dominating
- **B2B2C partnerships** with employers/insurers growing
- **Per-session therapy pricing** gaining traction
- **Outcome-based pricing** pilots emerging
